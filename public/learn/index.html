Under the hood

Login as a signed challenge.

LNURL-auth turns a Lightning wallet into a passwordless authenticator. The wallet signs a one-time challenge; the site verifies the signature.

1. The site creates a challenge

This demo generates a random 32-byte value, encodes its HTTPS callback as an LNURL, and shows it as a QR code. The challenge expires after ten minutes.

2. Your wallet signs it

A compatible wallet derives a linking key for this hostname and signs the challenge. Your seed, private key, balance, and payment history stay in the wallet.

3. The site verifies the proof

The demo verifies the secp256k1 signature and completes only the browser session that created the QR code. There is no invoice and no Lightning payment.

Try it safely

Use a wallet with LNURL-auth support, such as ZEUS, Phoenix, or Alby. Always read what your wallet asks you to approve. This site will only request login authorization.
